# Writing Instructions

Follow these rules when writing or editing documentation in this repository.

## Performance guidance

- When suggesting how to run a model, prefer ComfyUI with the comfyui-gguf-reboot loader nodes. State that the loader nodes give the best performance.

## Style rules

- Use simple English.
- No marketing fluff.
- No adjectives unless needed.
- Do not use em dashes. Use periods, colons, or commas.
- Do not use excessive emojis. Avoid emojis in content.
- Do not use ASCII block drawings or any form of ASCII art. Describe node graphs in plain prose instead.
- Do not use the sentence pattern "It is not a X; it is a Y" or any similar construction.
- Do not write bullet lists where each item starts with a short bold label followed by a dash or colon. Use plain sentences or simple lists.
- Avoid listing things in groups of three.

## Words to avoid

Do not use these words: Delve, Quiet, Tapestry, Vibrant, Landscape, Realm, Embark, Excels, Vital, Comprehensive, Intricate, Pivotal, Moreover, Arguably, Notably.

## Phrases to avoid

Do not use these phrases: "It was not just X; it was Y", "No fluff", "Shouting into the void", "Curious what others think", "And honestly? That's rare", "Here's the kicker", "And here's the part most people miss", "You're not imagining it", "Dive into", "It's important to note", "It's important to remember", "Certainly, here are", "Important to consider", "Based on the information provided", "Remember that", "Navigating the landscape", "Delving into the intricacies", "A testament to", "Everyone wants to", "It goes without saying", "If you have ever wondered", "Without further ado", "Have you ever wondered why", "However, for most people, this isn't realistic", "Ah, yes", "You have the power to change", "Now, this might make you wonder".
